# Indirekte Rede: Konjunktiv I vs. Ersatzform — C1

Standalone drill set, not tied to any lesson. Konjunktiv I only works as a marker of reported speech where it's actually distinct from the Indikativ — reliably true for 3rd person singular (er komme, er habe, er sei) but not for wir/sie(Pl.), where most verbs' Konjunktiv I form is identical to the Indikativ (wir kommen = wir kommen). When that happens, German switches to Konjunktiv II instead: sie kämen, sie hätten — or, for weak verbs whose Konjunktiv II also collides with their own Präteritum, to würde + Infinitiv (sie würden fordern). sein is the one verb whose plural Konjunktiv I (seien) stays distinct, so no swap is needed there. This set also covers reported yes/no and w-questions (ob/wie/wann + verb-final) and reported commands (sollen + Infinitiv). Blocks: A Basistraining → B Vertiefung → C Sprachbausteine → D Wiederholung. Answers in solutions.md.
