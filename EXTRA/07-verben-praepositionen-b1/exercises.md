# Erweiterte Verbliste & da-/wo-Komposita — B1

Standalone drill set, not tied to any lesson. Builds on the A2 set with more fixed-preposition verbs — including ones that take a Dativ preposition (Angst haben VOR, träumen VON) rather than Akkusativ — and the matching da(r)-/wo(r)- compounds: wovor/davor, wovon/davon, alongside worüber/darüber and wofür/dafür from before. The rule is the same regardless of case: a compound only replaces a THING, never a person. Blocks: A Basistraining → B Vertiefung → C Sprachbausteine → D Wiederholung. Answers in solutions.md.
