## Übung D2 — Odd one out

1. ~~neueren~~ — neueren is an attributive adjective form — doesn't belong with the predicative comparative group.
2. ~~am schöneren~~ — am schöneren is an invalid form — superlatives use -sten, not -eren.
3. ~~obwohl~~ — obwohl is a concessive conjunction — not a comparison word.
4. ~~mehr~~ — mehr is a quantity word — not a quality adjective describing a property.
5. ~~lieber~~ — lieber is an adverb/comparative — not a noun; all others are nouns.
6. ~~schnellste~~ — schnellste is an adjective form — all others are verbs.
