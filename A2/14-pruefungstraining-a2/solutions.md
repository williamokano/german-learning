## Aufgabe 7 — E-Mail ergänzen

| Gap | Answer | Grammar point |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **a) dir** | *gehen* takes Dativ: *Wie geht es dir?* — not *dich* (Akk.) |
| 2 | **a) ob** | indirect yes/no question (indirect question → `ob`) |
| 3 | **a) die** | Hotels, pl. Nom. relative pronoun = **die** |
| 4 | **a) weil** | reason → subordinate clause, verb at end (weil … liebe) |
| 5 | **b) denkst** | subject = *du* (singular informal) → 2nd person sg. = `denkst` |

**Aufgabe 7: ___ / 5 points**

⚠️ Gap 1: "Wie geht es dir?" is a fixed expression — `gehen` always takes Dativ for the experiencer.
⚠️ Gap 2: `ob` introduces indirect yes/no questions (will he have time?); `weil` introduces reason; `dass` introduces statements — only `ob` fits here.
⚠️ Gap 3: *Hotels* is plural → Nom. relative pronoun = `die` (not `das` = neuter sg., not `den` = mask. Akk.).
⚠️ Gap 4: The sentence "weil ich Kunst sehr liebe" has the verb at the end — correct `weil` structure. `Deshalb` would require inversion in a new main clause.
⚠️ Gap 5: The subject is "du" (second person informal) → present tense = `denkst`, not `denkt` (3rd person) or `denken` (plural/infinitive).
