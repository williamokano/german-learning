# A2 · Lektion 14 — Prüfungstraining A2 (Mock Exam)

**Format:** Goethe-Zertifikat A2 / telc Deutsch A2

This file is a **full mock exam** in the official Goethe-Zertifikat A2 / telc Deutsch A2
format. It covers all four exam skills: **Hören · Lesen · Schreiben · Sprechen**.

---

## How to use this mock exam

- Do the whole exam **in one sitting** under timed conditions.
- **Total time:** approximately 85 minutes (Hören 25 min · Lesen 30 min · Schreiben 20 min · Sprechen 10 min).
- Do **not** look at `solutions.md` until you have finished all four parts.
- The Sprechen part can be done with a partner, with a teacher, or self-recorded (monologue format).

**Scoring:** each item is worth 1 point unless otherwise stated. See `solutions.md` for
the full scoring grid and pass threshold.

---

# Teil 4 — Sprechen (Speaking)

**Instructions:** The speaking part is done with a partner (or recorded as a monologue). You have approximately 10 minutes total. Read the task card before you begin. Use complete sentences.

---

## Teil 4a — Sich vorstellen (Introduce yourself)

Talk about yourself for about 2 minutes. Cover these six points:

1. Name
2. Herkunft (Wo kommen Sie her? / Wo bist du geboren?)
3. Wohnort (Wo wohnen Sie / wohnst du jetzt?)
4. Beruf oder Studium (Was machen Sie / machst du beruflich?)
5. Sprachen (Welche Sprachen sprechen Sie / sprichst du?)
6. Freizeit (Was machen Sie / machst du in der Freizeit gern?)

---

## Teil 4c — Gemeinsam planen
