# Pavel Novák

- **Name:** Pavel Novák
- **Pronouns:** er / him
- **Age:** ~28
- **Nationality:** Czech
- **Currently lives in:** Berlin (Wedding) since 2023
- **Occupation:** Barista, training to become a specialty coffee roaster;
  works at a specialty café in Prenzlauer Berg
- **Languages:** Czech (native), German (B1, the reader's level), English
  (B1)
- **Family:** Younger brother of Tomáš Novák; parents still in Brno
- **Personality:** Quiet, observant, makes dry jokes. A good listener who
  occasionally asks the question that reframes the debate. Loves coffee
  and the science behind roasting. A1/A2 reader will recognize him from
  the brief appearance in A1/02 (Buchstabieren dialog with Frau Weber).
- **Voice:** `Pavel` key in `audio_config.json` → **Eric (Smooth,
  Trustworthy — calm, mid-register)**, speed 1.00
- **First appears in:** A1/02 (brief); full introduction in B1/02 as
  Tomáš's brother
- **Recurring in:** B1/02, B1/06, B1/08, B1/11
- **Notes:**
  - His voice is intentionally **distinct from Bruno's** so the two can
    appear in the same 3-person scene (B1/08: Anna + Mia + Pavel) without
    listener confusion.
  - The Pavel–Tomáš pair is a recurring "**contrasting brothers**" setup:
    Tomáš is energetic, mobile, EU-minded; Pavel is quiet, rooted, a
    listener. Useful for grammar lessons that need two opposing views
    (B1/02 Konnektoren, B1/08 Verben mit Präp).
