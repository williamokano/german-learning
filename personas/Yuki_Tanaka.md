# Yuki Tanaka

- **Name:** Yuki Tanaka
- **Pronouns:** sie / her
- **Age:** ~24
- **Nationality:** Japanese
- **Currently lives in:** Osaka, Japan (returned after A1/A2 German course in
  Berlin; B1 she's back in Japan and learning remotely)
- **Occupation:**
  - A1: international student in Berlin (German course at a VHS)
  - **B1**: Junior Software Engineer at a Tokyo company, learning German
    remotely with a tutor (B1 telc); considering a B2 course at the
    Goethe-Institut Tokyo
- **Languages:** Japanese (native), German (B1), English (B2)
- **Family:** Parents and a younger brother in Osaka; her mother often
  features in the A1 Hörtext (the "Yuki from Osaka" voice)
- **Personality:** Polite, careful, methodical. Asks a lot of questions
  (good for B1/04 KI debate and B1/09 phone-call drill). Always uses
  formal "Sie" with adults she doesn't know.
- **Voice:** `Yuki` key in `audio_config.json` → **Julia (Confident and
  Friendly, calm, focused)**, speed 1.00 in B1 (was 0.85 in A1)
- **First appears in:** A1 Hörtext (announcer voice reading her story in
  the listening passage)
- **Recurring in:**
  - A1: Hörtext in most lessons (announcer reads her story)
  - **B1: 04 (Anna + Lukas + Yuki video call about KI), 09 (Yuki calls a
    Berlin school from Osaka to ask about a B2 course)** — the B1
    re-introduces Yuki as a working professional rather than a student.
- **Notes:**
  - She is the only recurring character who lives outside Germany/Europe
    for most of B1.
  - In B1 she uses **the same `Julia` voice** as the announcer — but
    with a different speaker label so the audio script treats her as a
    speaker, not the announcer.
