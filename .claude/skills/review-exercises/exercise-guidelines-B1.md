# Exercise Patterns — B1

For grammar/vocabulary scope rules shared across all lesson reviews, see
`guidelines-B1.md`. **Always load both files** before reviewing.

This file defines what **exercise patterns** are appropriate at B1.

---

## Block structure

Same A1/A2 skeleton (34 exercises, ~180–220 items).

| Block | Exercises | Notes |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| H (Hören) | H1–H4 | Audio at natural speed (~180 wpm) |
| A (Basistraining) | A1–A12 | Präteritum + Plusquamperfekt + alle cases |
| B (Vertiefung) | B1–B9 | Subordinate clauses, passive, relatives |
| C (Prüfungstraining) | C1–C5 | telc B1 / Goethe B1 format |
| D (Wiederholung) | D1–D4 | Includes ≥20% from A1+A2 review |

## What's new at B1 vs A2

- **Präteritum** — narration in simple past (vs Perfekt in A1/A2)
- **Plusquamperfekt** — "Ich hatte das Buch schon gelesen"
- **All 4 cases productive** — Genitiv in exercises (nicht nur chunks)
- **Konjunktiv II real conditional** — "Wenn ich Zeit hätte, würde ich kommen"
- **Passive voice** (Vorgangspassiv) — "Das Auto wird gekauft"
- **Relative clauses (all cases)** — including Genitiv relatives
- **Indirect questions** — "Können Sie mir sagen, wo der Bahnhof ist?"
- **Two-part connectors** — `sowohl … als auch`, `weder … noch`, `entweder … oder`, `nicht nur … sondern auch`
- **Future-in-the-past** — "Ich würde das machen, wenn ich Zeit gehabt hätte"

## Exercise types at B1

### ✅ Use freely

All A1/A2 types plus:
- **Order with Konjunktiv II** — "Wenn ich Zeit hätte, würde ich kommen"
- **Order with passive** — "Das Auto wird gekauft"
- **Order with Präteritum of strong verbs** — `er ging`, `er aß`, `er fuhr`
- **Gap-text with passive** — Vorgangspassiv with `werden`
- **Gap-text with Genitiv** — `wegen des Regens`
- **Gap-text with relative clauses** — `der Mann, den ich kenne`
- **Gap-text with two-part connectors** — `sowohl … als auch` requires TWO gaps
- **Gap-text with Plusquamperfekt** — `hatte … gegessen` (auxiliary before past participle)

### ⚠️ Use with care

- **Long subordinate clauses** — keep ≤2 per sentence
- **Mixed Präteritum and Perfekt** in narration — establish the context first
- **Genitive in free-write** — students may substitute `von + Dativ`

### ❌ Avoid at B1

- Konjunktiv I (reported speech — B2)
- Partizip I/II as adjective (B2)
- Nominalisation chains (B2)
- Funktionsverbgefüge (B2)
- Academic register (B2)

## Gap-text patterns

### Sentence length

- **Recommended:** 10–18 words
- **Maximum:** 22 words
- **Subordinate clauses:** up to 2 per sentence

### Two-part connector exercises

When testing `sowohl … als auch`, `weder … noch`, etc.:

- **MUST** have TWO gaps, not one
- ❌ Bug: "Sie liebt {12} Berlin, sie kritisiert es" with answer "sowohl"
- ✅ Fix: "Sie liebt {12} Berlin, {13} sie kritisiert es" with answers "sowohl" and "aber"

### Passive exercises

- [ ] Vorgangspassiv uses `werden` (Präsens: `wird`; Präteritum: `wurde`)
- [ ] Zustandspassiv uses `sein` (only at B1+ as state-result)
- [ ] No `bekommen` + Passiv at B1 (B2)

### Genitive exercises

- [ ] `wegen` + Genitiv: `wegen des Regens` (NOT `wegen dem`)
- [ ] `trotz` + Genitiv: `trotz des Sturms` (NOT `trotz dem`)
- [ ] Genitive articles: `des` (masc/neut), `der` (fem), `der` (pl)

### Relative-clause exercises

- [ ] Verb at end of relative clause
- [ ] Case determined by role in relative clause (not antecedent's case)
- [ ] After preposition: `mit dem`, `für den`, `zu der`, `in dem`

## gap-bank patterns

### H3

- Same structure as A1/A2
- Bank may include `weil`/`dass`/`wenn`/`als`/`obwohl` as distractors

### C3

- 15 words, 10 gaps, 5 distractors
- Bank may include abstract nouns (B1 vocab) and discourse markers
- Cross-gap ambiguity check: no bank word fits multiple gaps

## Order-exercise patterns

### Tile count

- **B1 minimum:** 4 tiles
- **B1 maximum:** 8 tiles
- **Recommended:** 5–7 tiles

### Sentence structure

- All A1/A2 structures plus:
- **Subordinate clauses** — verb must be at end (especially `weil`, `dass`, `obwohl`, `wenn`, `als`, `nachdem`, `bevor`)
- **Konjunktiv II** — "Ich würde gern…", "Wenn ich Zeit hätte…"
- **Passive** — "Das Buch wird gelesen"
- **Präteritum of strong verbs** — `er ging`, `er aß`, `er fuhr`, `er las`, `er half`
- **Präteritum of modals** — `er konnte`, `er musste`, `er wollte`, `er durfte`, `er sollte`, `er mochte`

### Verbs in tiles

- All conjugated
- Exception: Passive infinitive (Partizip II) at end of clause
- Exception: Modalverb Satzklammer (conjugated modal + infinitive)
- Exception: Perfekt (auxiliary + past participle)
- Exception: Präteritum narration (all in Präteritum form)

## Free-write patterns (B1 standard)

- **Length:** 6–8 sentences
- **Stimulus:** formal E-Mail or forum post (per AUTHORING-V2.md B9 spec)
- **Use checklist:** 3–4 bullet items
- **Self-check:** 3–4 form checks (case endings, verb position, etc.)
- **Model answer:** present, demonstrates all required structures

## Common B1 exercise-schema errors to flag

1. **`obwohl` clause with verb not at end** — "Obwohl ich bin müde" → "Obwohl ich müde bin"
2. **Two-part connector with only one gap** — needs TWO gaps
3. **Passive with wrong auxiliary** — process = `werden`, state = `sein`
4. **Präteritum of strong verb wrong** — `er ging` not `er gehte`
5. **Präteritum of modals wrong** — `er konnte` (no umlaut, no -t)
6. **Genitive with `wegen`/`trotz` in Dativ** — A2 habit, not acceptable at B1
7. **Relative clause with wrong case after preposition** — `mit dem` (Dat) not `mit den`
8. **Indirect question with V2** — "Können Sie mir sagen, wo ist der Bahnhof?" → "Können Sie mir sagen, wo der Bahnhof ist?"

---

## CEFR-appropriate distractors

Distractors at B1 should:

- Be in A1+A2+B1 vocabulary range
- Include common learner errors (e.g. wrong Präteritum forms)
- Be plausible paraphrases
- Include discourse markers (allerdings, trotzdem, deshalb) as distractors for connector gaps
- NOT include B2 academic vocabulary

## Example: acceptable vs unacceptable B1 exercises

| ❌ Unacceptable at B1 | Why | ✅ Acceptable alternative |
|------------------------|-----|--------------------------|
| Order: [obwohl, ich, müde, bin, gehe, ich, arbeiten] | `obwohl` clause verb not at end | Order: [obwohl, ich, müde, bin, gehe, ich, arbeiten, .] with bin at end — but the tiles are out of order; need 8 tiles for the clause+main |
| Gap-text: "{1} das Auto wird gekauft." with answer "weil" | `weil` doesn't fit "wird gekauft" | Restructure: "Das Auto wird gekauft, {1} es billig ist." with answer "weil" |
| C3 distractor: "GESELLSCHAFT" (B2 word) | B2 vocabulary | Use B1 vocab: "ERFAHRUNG", "MEINUNG" (acceptable at B1) |
| Passive: "Das Buch **ist** gelesen **worden**." | Double passive | "Das Buch **wird** gelesen." (process) or "Das Buch **ist** gelesen." (state) |
| Two-part connector: "Sie mag {1} Kaffee **als auch** Tee." with answer "sowohl" | Wrong connector pair | "Sie mag {1} Kaffee, {2} Tee." with answers "sowohl" and "als" |
