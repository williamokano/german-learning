# Exercise Patterns — A2

For grammar/vocabulary scope rules shared across all lesson reviews, see
`guidelines-A2.md`. **Always load both files** before reviewing.

This file defines what **exercise patterns** (types, distractor styles, gap
complexity, block sizes) are appropriate at A2.

## What's new at A2 vs A1

- **Subordinate clauses appear in exercises** — `weil`, `dass`, `wenn`
- **Modal verb Satzklammer** — exercises test "Ich möchte Deutsch lernen" (conjugated in V2, infinitive at end)
- **Reflexive verbs in Dat** — exercises include `sich etwas vorstellen`, `sich Sorgen machen`
- **Two-way prepositions** — Dat (location) vs Akk (direction)
- **Comparative / superlative** — exercises test `genauso…wie`, `-er`/`-ste` forms
- **Adjective endings** — after `der`/`ein` (masc, fem, neut, pl)
- **Konjunktiv II polite** — `Ich würde gern…`, `Ich hätte gern…`
- **Futur I** — `Ich werde morgen…`
- **Relative clauses** (Nom/Akk only) — `der Mann, den ich kenne`

## Exercise types at A2

### ✅ Use freely

All A1 types plus:
- **Order with subordinate clauses** — `weil ich müde bin` (verb-final)
- **Order with modalverb Satzklammer** — `[möchte, ich, lernen, Deutsch]` → "Ich möchte Deutsch lernen"
- **Gap-text with reflexive Dat** — `Ich wasche mir die Hände`
- **Gap-text with subordinate clauses** — `Ich bleibe zu Hause, weil ich müde bin`
- **Multiple-gap text** with 10+ gaps
- **Complex single-choice** with paraphrase distractors

### ⚠️ Use with care

- **Order with separable verb + subordinate** — easy to get the prefix position wrong
- **Long gap-text with 2+ subordinate clauses** — keep sentences ≤15 words
- **Free-write with reflective content** — A2 still needs scaffolding

### ❌ Avoid at A2

- Konjunktiv I (reported speech — B2)
- Genitive as productive grammar (only chunks)
- Passive voice (any form)
- Past subjunctive as conditional (B1)

## Gap-text patterns

### Sentence length

- **Recommended:** 8–15 words
- **Maximum:** 18 words
- **Subordinate clauses OK** — keep to 1 per sentence

### Subordinate-clause verification

For every `weil`/`dass`/`wenn`/`als`/`obwohl` clause:

- [ ] Verb is at the end of the clause
- [ ] The clause is separated by a comma from the main clause
- [ ] The verb in the main clause is still in V2 position

### Gap count per exercise

- **Recommended:** 8–12 gaps
- **Maximum:** 15 gaps

---

## gap-bank patterns

### H3

- Same as A1: 6–9 words, 0–3 distractors
- Now may include subordinate-clause distractors (e.g. `weil` as a distractor when gap is a conjunction)

### C3

- 15 words, 10 gaps, 5 distractors
- Now may include subordinate-clause conjunctions as distractors (`weil`, `dass`, `wenn`, `als`)
- Cross-gap ambiguity check: no bank word fits multiple gaps
- `bankCase: upper`, `bankSort: true`

## Order-exercise patterns

### Tile count

- **A2 minimum:** 3 tiles
- **A2 maximum:** 7 tiles
- **Recommended:** 4–6 tiles

### Sentence structure

- Statements, yes/no questions, W-questions (same as A1)
- **NEW:** subordinate clauses — verb must be at end
- **NEW:** modal verb Satzklammer — conjugated in V2, infinitive at end
- **NEW:** Perfekt narration — `[bin, ich, gestern, gegangen, nach, Hause]` → "Ich bin gestern nach Hause gegangen"

### Verbs in tiles

- All conjugated (no infinitive)
- Exception: Perfekt (auxiliary + past participle)
- Exception: Modalverb Satzklammer (conjugated modal + infinitive)
- NEW: Future I (`werde` + infinitive) — only at A2/12+

- Same as A1, but distractors may include abstract A2 vocab (e.g. *die Erfahrung*, *die Meinung*)

## Categorize patterns

- 12–16 tokens
- 2–4 buckets
- **NEW:** grammatical categorisations (z.B. Verben/Nomen/Adjektive, Perfekt/Präteritum)

## Free-write patterns (A2 standard)

- **Length:** 4–6 sentences
- **Stimulus:** WhatsApp/E-Mail/Chatnachricht/Forum (per AUTHORING-V2.md B9 spec)
- **Use checklist:** 3–4 bullet items
- **Self-check:** 3–4 form checks
- **Model answer:** present, with key phrases

## Common A2 exercise-schema errors to flag

1. **Order with subordinate clause but verb not at end** — verb position error
2. **Modal verb Satzklammer with infinitive in wrong position** — infinitive must be at end
3. **Reflexive Dat confused with Akk** — `sich` in wrong case
4. **Two-way preposition with wrong case** — location vs direction
5. **Konjunktiv II forms** — `wäret` is archaic; `wärt` is correct ihr-form
6. **Adjective endings after `kein`** — A2 covers definite + indefinite but not `kein` mixed (B2)
7. **`als` vs `wenn` for single past** — `als ich 5 war` not `wenn ich 5 war`

---

## CEFR-appropriate distractors

Distractors at A2 should:

- Be in A1+A2 vocabulary range
- Include common learner errors (e.g. wrong reflexive case)
- Be plausible paraphrases of correct answers
- NOT include abstract B2 vocabulary (*die Implementierung*, *die Verantwortung* in distractors)

## Example: acceptable vs unacceptable A2 exercises

| ❌ Unacceptable at A2 | Why | ✅ Acceptable alternative |
|------------------------|-----|--------------------------|
| Order: [ich, gehe, weil, müde, bin, "?"] | `weil` clause with verb not at end (bin should be last) | Order: [ich, gehe, weil, müde, "?"] with bin removed (or restructured to avoid weil in order) |
| Gap-text: "{1} das Auto gefällt mich." | Wrong case after gefallen (Dat, not Akk) | "Das Auto gefällt {1}." → answer "mir" |
| C3 distractor: "KÖNNTEN" | Konjunktiv II polite is OK, but KÖNNTEN as distractor for non-Konjunktiv gap | Use KÖNNTEN only in gap 9-10 if teaching Konjunktiv II |
| Gap-text with subordinate clause: "Ich denke, dass mein Bruder {1} morgen kommt." with answer "kommt" | `dass` clause with verb at position 2 | "Ich denke, dass mein Bruder morgen {1}." with answer "kommt" (verb at end) |
| Order: [ich, wasche, die, Hände, mir] → "Ich wasche mir die Hände" | Correct! But check for `sich wasche` (Akk reflexive) variants | Both `wasche mir die Hände` and `wasche die Hände` are valid; pick the Dat variant if teaching body parts |
