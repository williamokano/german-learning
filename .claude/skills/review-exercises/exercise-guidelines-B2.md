For grammar/vocabulary scope rules shared across all lesson reviews, see

## Block structure

Same skeleton (34 exercises, ~180–220 items).

| Block | Exercises | Notes |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| H (Hören) | H1–H4 | Natural audio; topics B2-relevant (work, society) |

from English to German at B2).

## What's new at B2 vs B1

- **Konjunktiv I** — reported speech: "Er sagte, er habe Zeit"
- **Konjunktiv II past** — "Ich hätte das machen sollen", "Wenn ich Zeit gehabt hätte, wäre ich gekommen"
- **Partizip I as adjective** — "der lesende Mann"
- **Partizip II as adjective** — "das gekaufte Auto"
- **Funktionsverbgefüge** — "in Frage stellen", "zur Verfügung stehen"
- **Nominalstil** — "Die Verarbeitung der Daten ist wichtig"
- **Wortbildung** — Nominalisation, compounds, derivation
- **Wissenschaft und Technik** — academic register (light)
- **Wirtschaft und Arbeitswelt** — professional register

## Exercise types at B2

### ✅ Use freely

All A1/A2/B1 types plus:
- **Order with Konjunktiv I** — "Er sagte, er habe Zeit"
- **Order with Konjunktiv II past** — "Ich hätte das gern gemacht"
- **Order with Funktionsverbgefüge** — "Diese Frage stellt man in Frage" (verb-final)
- **Order with Partizip I/II as adjective** — "Das ist der lesende Mann"
- **Order with Passive alternatives** — `man` + Aktiv, `sein` + Zustandspassiv, `bekommen` + Passiv
- **Gap-text with complex nominal phrases** — "Die Verarbeitung der Daten ist wichtig"
- **Gap-text with academic register** — light academic vocabulary
- **Long complex order exercises** — 7–9 tiles

### ⚠️ Use with care

- **Konjunktiv I 1st/3rd sg** — identical to Indikativ; use Konjunktiv II fallback or 3rd pl
- **Heavy nominalisation chains** — B2 allows 2–3 attributes; longer is C1
- **Modalverben subjektiv** — "Er soll König gewesen sein" (rumour) — B2 specific

### ❌ Avoid at B2

- Academic research register (C1)
- Heavy literary register
- Pure dialect

## Gap-text patterns

### Sentence length

- **Recommended:** 12–25 words
- **Maximum:** 30 words
- **Subordinate clauses:** up to 2 per sentence

### Passive alternatives

- [ ] Vorgangspassiv: `werden` + Partizip II
- [ ] Zustandspassiv: `sein` + Partizip II (state, not process)
- [ ] `man` + Aktiv: when agent is generic
- [ ] `bekommen` + Passiv: recipient passive ("Ich bekomme ein Buch geschenkt")

### Konjunktiv I exercises

- [ ] 3rd person singular: `er habe`, `er sei`, `er werde` (Konjunktiv I — same as Indikativ in spoken)
- [ ] 3rd person plural: `sie haben` (Konjunktiv I — same as Indikativ!) — use Konjunktiv II fallback: `sie hätten` for unambiguous reported speech
- [ ] 1st/3rd sg indistinguishable from Indikativ — pedagogical note: use 3rd pl or paraphrase

### Partizip I/II as adjective

- [ ] Position: before the noun (attributive)
- [ ] Declension: weak after `der`/`dieser`; strong after `ein`; mixed after `kein` (B2)
- [ ] Example: `der lesende Mann` (Partizip I) / `das gekaufte Auto` (Partizip II)

### Funktionsverbgefüge

- [ ] Verb-final position: "Diese Frage ist in Frage zu stellen"
- [ ] Noun + preposition + verb form: "in Frage stellen", "zur Verfügung stehen"
- [ ] Distinguish action vs state: "zur Verfügung **stellen**" (action) vs "zur Verfügung **stehen**" (state)

## gap-bank patterns

### H3

- Same structure; bank may include B2 abstract nouns
- Distractor plausibility: more sophisticated distractors (paraphrases, near-synonyms)

### C3

- 15 words, 10 gaps, 5 distractors
- Bank may include Konjunktiv I/II forms, Partizip I/II forms, Funktionsverbgefüge components
- Cross-gap ambiguity check: especially important at B2 because forms are more flexible

## Order-exercise patterns

### Tile count

- **B2 minimum:** 5 tiles
- **B2 maximum:** 9 tiles
- **Recommended:** 6–8 tiles

### Sentence structure

- All A1/A2/B1 structures plus:
- **Konjunktiv I reported speech** — `er habe`, `er sei`, `er werde` (and Konjunktiv II fallback for 3rd pl)
- **Konjunktiv II past** — `hätte + Modalverb + Infinitiv` (e.g. "Ich hätte das machen können")
- **Passive alternatives** — `werden` + Partizip II (Vorgangspassiv); `sein` + Partizip II (Zustandspassiv)
- **Funktionsverbgefüge** — verb-final position
- **Partizip I/II as adjective** — declined correctly before the noun
- **Modalverben subjektiv** — "Er soll König gewesen sein" (rumour)

### Verbs in tiles

- All conjugated
- Exception: Passive infinitive (Partizip II) at end of clause
- Exception: Modalverb Satzklammer (conjugated modal + infinitive)
- Exception: Perfekt / Plusquamperfekt
- Exception: Konjunktiv I/II forms as conjugated verbs
- **NEW:** Konjunktiv II past of modals: "hätte ... machen können"

## Free-write patterns (B2 standard)

- **Length:** 8–10 sentences
- **Stimulus:** formal E-Mail, opinion piece, or essay
- **Use checklist:** 3–4 bullet items including passive and Konjunktiv
- **Self-check:** 4 form checks
- **Model answer:** present, demonstrates all required structures

## Common B2 exercise-schema errors to flag

1. **Konjunktiv I 3rd sg used as if it were Indikativ** — pedagogical gap
2. **Zustandspassiv vs Vorgangspassiv confusion** — wrong auxiliary
3. **`bekommen` + Passiv without Dativ recipient** — `Ich bekomme geschenkt` (wrong) vs `Ich bekomme ein Buch geschenkt` (correct)
4. **Funktionsverbgefüge verb not at end** — "Diese Frage stellt in Frage" (wrong) vs "Diese Frage stellt man in Frage" (correct, but still wrong order — should be "in Frage zu stellen")
5. **Partizip I/II inflection wrong** — `der lesend Mann` (wrong) vs `der lesende Mann` (correct)
6. **Nominalisation wrong gender** — `die Lesen` (wrong) vs `das Lesen` (correct, neuter)
7. **Modalverben subjektiv with wrong Konjunktiv** — "Er **sollte** König gewesen sein" (wrong) vs "Er **soll** König gewesen sein" (correct)
8. **Reported speech tense confusion** — "Er sagte, er **hatte** Zeit" (wrong) vs "Er sagte, er **habe** Zeit" (correct)

## CEFR-appropriate distractors

Distractors at B2 should:

- Be in A1+A2+B1+B2 vocabulary range
- Include common B2 learner errors (wrong auxiliary in passive, wrong Konjunktiv form)
- Include paraphrase distractors (e.g. active vs passive paraphrase)
- NOT include C1 academic-research vocabulary

## Example: acceptable vs unacceptable B2 exercises

| ❌ Unacceptable at B2 | Why | ✅ Acceptable alternative |
|------------------------|-----|--------------------------|
| Order: [er, sagte, er, hat, Zeit] | Reported speech without Konjunktiv I | Order: [er, sagte, er, habe, Zeit] (Konjunktiv I 3rd sg) — or for 3rd pl: "er **hätte** Zeit" (Konjunktiv II fallback) |
| Gap-text: "Das Buch **ist** gekauft **worden**." | Double passive | "Das Buch **wird** gekauft." (process) or "Das Buch **ist** gekauft." (state) |
| C3 distractor: "IMPLEMENTIERUNG" (C1 word) | Out of scope | Use B2 vocab: "UMSETZUNG", "VERWIRKLICHUNG" |
| Order: [Diese, Frage, stellt, man, in, Frage] → "Diese Frage stellt man in Frage" | Wrong word order (FVG verb-final) | Order: [Diese, Frage, ist, in, Frage, zu, stellen] (formal) or [Man, stellt, diese, Frage, in, Frage] (informal) |
| Partizip: "Der **lesend** Mann" | Wrong inflection | "Der **lesende** Mann" (Partizip I + weak adjective ending) |
