# CEFR Guidelines — A2 (Waystage)

**CEFR descriptor:** Can understand sentences and frequently used expressions
related to areas of most immediate relevance (e.g. very basic personal and
family information, shopping, local geography, employment). Can communicate in
simple and routine tasks requiring a simple and direct exchange of information
on familiar and routine matters. Can describe in simple terms aspects of his/her
background, immediate environment and matters in areas of immediate need.

### Vocabulary

- Extended family (Schwiegermutter, Cousine, Neffe)
- Daily routine verbs (aufwachen, sich anziehen, sich fertig machen)
- Travel (Flug, Gepäck, Buchung, Unterkunft)
- Health (Erkältung, Fieber, Rezept, Apotheke)
- Work (Bewerbung, Lebenslauf, Vorstellungsgespräch)
- Media (Handy, Internet, WLAN)
- Comparisons (genauso, doppelt so viel)
- Modal particles: doch, mal, ja (productive)

### Can-do statements

- Tell a story in Perfekt (weekend, vacation, yesterday)
- Describe apartment layout and household rules
- Give reasons using `weil` and `dass`
- Talk about work, write a short CV
- Compare products and places
- Describe people in detail (clothes, personality, appearance)
- Make polite requests and wishes
- Plan a trip (booking, hotel, asking for directions)
- Talk about future plans (Futur I)
- Describe someone with a relative clause

## A2 Should-NOT-Have (out of scope — flag Critical if found)

### Grammar

- ❌ **Konjunktiv I** (still B2+; reported speech not until B2)
- ❌ **Konjunktiv II unreal past** (e.g. *„Ich hätte das machen sollen"* = B1)
- ❌ **Passive voice** (any form)
- ❌ **Genitive case** as productive grammar (only as chunk like *des Herrn*)
- ❌ **Participles as adjectives** (Partizip I/II as attribute = B2)
- ❌ **Genitive relatives** (whose, in which — B1)
- ❌ **Infinitive clauses with `um…zu`, `ohne…zu`, `statt…zu`** (B1+)
- ❌ **Two-way prepositions with abstract motion** (B1 for figurative uses)
- ❌ **`obwohl`/`trotzdem` clauses** (B1/02)
- ❌ **Past subjunctive as conditional** (*Wenn ich Zeit hätte…* = B1)
- ❌ **Plusquamperfekt** (B1/01)
- ❌ **Nominalisation** of verbs (B2+)
- ❌ **Adjective declension after `kein`/`mein`/`dein` mixed** (B2 — A2 covers definite + indefinite only)
- ❌ **`dass` vs `wenn` confusion in object clauses** (acceptable chunks only)
- ❌ **Complex connectors** (`einerseits…andererseits`, `sowohl…als auch` — B1)

### Vocabulary

- ❌ Abstract philosophical terms (Gesinnung, Weltanschauung)
- ❌ Fachsprache (medical, legal, technical jargon)
- ❌ Stylistic register variants beyond colloquial/formal

### Register

- ❌ Formal-academic register
- ❌ Heavy dialect (no Bayerisch/Sächsisch except as cultural aside)

## Common learner errors at A2

1. **`weil` clause verb position** — students put the verb at position 2
   - ❌ „Ich bleibe zu Hause, weil ich bin müde."
   - ✅ „Ich bleibe zu Hause, weil ich müde bin."
2. **Dative pronouns after dative verbs** — wrong case
   - ❌ „Das Auto gefällt mich."
   - ✅ „Das Auto gefällt mir."
3. **Adjective endings after `ein`** — students omit the ending
   - ❌ „Ich habe ein klein Bruder." (masc nom)
   - ✅ „Ich habe einen kleinen Bruder."
4. **Word order in subordinate clauses** — `dass` clauses don't trigger V2
   - ❌ „Ich weiß, dass er kommt morgen."
   - ✅ „Ich weiß, dass er morgen kommt."
5. **`als` vs `wenn`** — students overgeneralize `wenn`
   - ❌ „**Wenn** ich 5 Jahre alt war, bin ich nach Berlin gezogen." (single past)
   - ✅ „**Als** ich 5 Jahre alt war, bin ich nach Berlin gezogen."
6. **Two-way prepositions motion vs location** — students use Akkusativ for static location
   - ❌ „Ich bin **in den** Park." (motion) vs „Ich bin **im** Park." (location)
   - ❌ (at A2 we expect both: "Ich gehe **in den** Park" + "Ich bin **im** Park")
7. **Reflexive pronouns in Dativ** — confusing *sich* (Akk) vs *sich* (Dat) when there's also an object
   - ❌ „Ich wasche **mich** die Hände." (Akk only — wrong because there's a Dat object)
   - ✅ „Ich wasche **mir** die Hände." (Dat reflexive for body part)
8. **Konjunktiv II word order** — students forget verb-final
   - ❌ „Ich würde gern ich komme mit."
   - ✅ „Ich würde gern mitkommen."
9. **`werden` vs `bekommen`** — passive vs *get passive* — A2 uses active only
   - ❌ „Ich **bekomme** geschenkt." (with a gift — wrong at A2)
   - ✅ „Ich **bekomme** ein Geschenk." (active, no passivation)

## Cross-language interference at A2

| L1              | Predictable A2 errors |
|-----------------|------------------------|
| English         | Word order in subordinate clauses (English uses V2 everywhere); false friends (sensible = vernünftig) |
| Portuguese      | Adjective endings (Portuguese also has them but rules differ); `als/wenn` |
| Spanish         | Perfect tense (Spanish uses Present Perfect for recent past, German uses Präteritum for narrative); `sich` vs `sich` (Dat/Akk) |
| French          | `sich` (Dat) — French doesn't have this distinction; gender agreement for borrowed words |

## Example: acceptable vs unacceptable A2 sentences

| ❌ Unacceptable at A2 | Why | ✅ Acceptable alternative |
|------------------------|-----|--------------------------|
| „Ich **hätte** das machen **sollen**." | Konjunktiv II unreal past = B1 | „Ich **hätte** das machen **sollen**." (acceptable at B1) — at A2 prefer: „Ich **sollte** das machen." (Präteritum of modal) |
| „**Obwohl** ich müde bin, gehe ich arbeiten." | *obwohl* clause = B1/02 | „Ich bin müde, **aber** ich gehe arbeiten." (use *aber* coordination) |
| „Das Buch, **das** ich gelesen **habe**…" | Relative clause = A2/13 (acceptable at A2/13 onward!) | At A2/13: „Das Buch, **das** ich gelesen **habe**, war gut." — earlier A2 lessons: avoid relatives |
| „Ich **werde** morgen arbeiten." | Futur I = A2/12 (acceptable at A2/12 onward!) | At A2/12: „Ich **werde** morgen arbeiten." — earlier A2: „Ich **arbeite** morgen." |
| „Das Auto **wurde** gekauft." | Passive = B1 | „Ich **habe** das Auto gekauft." (active Perfekt) |
| „**Deshalb** ich lerne Deutsch." | Connectors don't disrupt V2 | „**Deshalb** lerne ich Deutsch." |
