## Übung B3 — Richtigen Konnektor wählen

1. **b) obwohl** — obwohl introduces a subordinate clause (verb-final). trotzdem would need a new main clause.
2. **c) Trotzdem** — trotzdem (adverb) connects two main clauses expressing contrast. obwohl would need a Nebensatz.
3. **a) Deshalb** — deshalb introduces a consequence. The previous sentence gives the reason.
4. **a) zwar … aber** — zwar ... aber is the two-part concession structure.
5. **b) Dennoch** — dennoch (= trotzdem, more formal) contrasts the obstacle with the determined action.
6. **c) Deswegen** — deswegen states the consequence of the high demand.
