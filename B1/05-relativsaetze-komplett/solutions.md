## Übung B3 — Richtiges Relativpronomen wählen

1. **a) die** — die App (f.) as direct object → Akkusativ feminine → die.
2. **b) denen** — Leute (pl.) with dative verb helfen → Dativ plural → denen.
3. **c) in dem** — das Stadtgebiet (n.) + preposition in → Dativ → in dem.
4. **a) was** — das Wichtigste is a neuter superlative → relative pronoun is was.
5. **b) wo** — wo is the standard relative pronoun after place nouns (Stadt, Ort, Land…).
6. **c) für den** — der Grund (m.) + für (Akkusativ) → für den.

## Übung D2 — Welches Wort passt nicht?

1. ~~denen~~ — denen is the Dativ plural form; der/die/das are Nominativ/Akkusativ forms (denen does not appear in Nom. or Akk.).
2. ~~das Auto~~ — das Auto is a specific neuter noun (→ relative pronoun das); alles/etwas/nichts all trigger was.
3. ~~worüber~~ — worüber is a Pronominaladverb (B1/08 topic); the others are preposition + relative pronoun (the B1/05 form).
4. ~~die Nachfrage~~ — die Nachfrage is not a place noun; der Ort / die Stadt / das Land all take `wo` as a relative pronoun.
5. ~~gefallen~~ — gefallen takes Dativ but no preposition; the other three verbs all take a preposition that appears in relative clauses.
6. ~~denen~~ — denen is a relative pronoun; die Formulierung / der Hinweis / das Merkmal are nouns (Wortschatz from this lesson).
