# Verben mit festem Kasus: helfen, danken, gefallen, gehören … — A1

Standalone drill set, not tied to any lesson. Some German verbs simply require a fixed case, and you have to learn it with the verb — it has nothing to do with meaning. helfen, danken, gefallen, gehören always take the Dativ; sehen, kennen always take the Akkusativ. This set drills that with full noun phrases (not just pronouns), so the case shows on the article, not just on a pronoun form. Blocks: A Basistraining → B Vertiefung → C Sprachbausteine → D Wiederholung. Answers in solutions.md.
