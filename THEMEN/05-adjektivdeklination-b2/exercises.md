# Adjektive nach Quantoren & Partizipien als Adjektive — B2

Standalone drill set, not tied to any lesson. Closes out the Adjektivdeklination topic with two pieces: quantifiers before a Plural noun split into two groups — alle/beide act like a der-word, so the adjective after them takes the WEAK ending -en (alle guten Freunde); viele/einige/wenige/manche don't, so the adjective takes the STRONG ending -e in Nominativ/Akkusativ (viele gute Freunde, not viele guten Freunde) — and Partizip I (-end, "the laughing man") / Partizip II ("the written letter") used as adjectives, which decline exactly like any other adjective once formed. Blocks: A Basistraining → B Vertiefung → C Sprachbausteine → D Wiederholung — a full-topic mix. Answers in solutions.md.
