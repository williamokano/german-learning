# Indirekte Rede: Konjunktiv I — B2

Standalone drill set, not tied to any lesson. Formal written German reports what someone said using Konjunktiv I, not just "dass" + Indikativ: Er sagt, er SEI müde (not: er ist müde) — it signals "this is a claim, not a fact I'm vouching for," which is exactly why newspapers use it. sein is fully irregular (sei, seist, sei, seien, seiet, seien); most other verbs just add -e to the stem for er/sie/es: er habe, er komme, er gehe, er könne, er müsse. This set sticks to the common, unambiguous 3rd-person-singular case. Blocks: A Basistraining → B Vertiefung → C Sprachbausteine → D Wiederholung. Answers in solutions.md.
