# Adjektivendungen ohne Artikel, im Genitiv & mehrere Adjektive — B1

Standalone drill set, not tied to any lesson. Builds on the A2 set with what's left: the Nullartikel / "starke" declension — no article at all (frischer Fisch, kaltes Wasser), where the adjective carries the FULL der-word ending, including a Plural -e that is easy to confuse with the -en you learned after der/ein (gute Kinder, not guten Kinder — Nullartikel plural is the one place -e, not -en, shows up); the Genitiv adjective ending (-en almost everywhere, -er only in feminin/Plural); and multiple adjectives in a row, which all take the SAME ending. Blocks: A Basistraining → B Vertiefung → C Sprachbausteine → D Wiederholung. Answers in solutions.md.
