# je … desto, indem, sodass, falls, sofern, es sei denn — B2

Standalone drill set, not tied to any lesson. Closes out the Nebensatzkonnektoren topic with six advanced connectors: je … desto (the je-clause is verb-final, the desto-clause has normal Verb-Zweit with desto+comparative in position 1); indem (method — "by doing X") vs. sodass (result — "with the consequence that"), easy to mix up since both translate loosely as "so that" in English; falls/sofern (both "if/provided that", falls more everyday, sofern more formal); and es sei denn ("unless"), which — unlike every other connector here — is followed by a normal Verb-Zweit clause, not a Nebensatz. Blocks: A Basistraining → B Vertiefung → C Sprachbausteine → D Wiederholung — a full-topic mix. Answers in solutions.md.
