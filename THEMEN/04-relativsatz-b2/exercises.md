# Relativsätze: Genitiv (dessen/deren) — B2

Standalone drill set, not tied to any lesson. Closes out the Relativsätze topic with the Genitiv relative pronoun: dessen (after a maskulin or neutral antecedent) and deren (after a feminin or Plural antecedent) — "whose". Unlike der/die/das, dessen/deren never change for the case of the following noun's own role, and NO article ever follows them (der Mann, dessen Auto — not dessen das Auto). Blocks: A Basistraining (full capstone table, then dessen/deren) → B Vertiefung → C Sprachbausteine → D Wiederholung — a full-topic mix. Answers in solutions.md.
