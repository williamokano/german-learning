# Unregelmäßige Nomen, Eigennamen, kein-Wörter & Genitivketten — B2

Standalone drill set, not tied to any lesson. Closes out the Deklination topic with what's left after A2/B1: irregular weak nouns (der Name -> des Namens, not des Namen; das Herz, a neuter that mixes in weak endings in Dativ/Genitiv but stays unchanged in Akkusativ), Genitiv of proper names (Peters, Annas, Max' — apostrophe, not -s, after a name ending in s/x/z), kein across all cases, and Genitiv chains (das Auto des Bruders meiner Freundin). Answers in solutions.md.
